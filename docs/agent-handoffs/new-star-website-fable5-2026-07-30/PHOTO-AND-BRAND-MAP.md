# Photo and brand asset map

## 1. Rules

Use real New Star work strategically where it reduces uncertainty. Do not turn the site into an indiscriminate gallery.

For every image:

- preserve truthful before/after pairing and orientation;
- never imply two unrelated photos are the same surface;
- do not invent a city, customer, service, date, or result detail;
- use factual alt text and short visible captions;
- retain a rights/permission record outside the public repository;
- strip location-bearing EXIF from any newly added public image;
- create appropriately sized WebP/AVIF derivatives through a reproducible script;
- check focus, color, crop, LCP impact, and mobile composition;
- avoid using a weak or visibly worn “after” as the main premium hero.

The user's statement establishes that the working photos are business before/after and clean-space images. It does not establish model/property releases or a license ledger. Do not infer those from filenames.
